the map

We built the map so you can see who's out tonight, and we built it to give away as little as we could. Your face sits somewhere within a kilometre of you and keeps moving on its own. The map only zooms in so far. No exact location is ever stored. Everyone you see on it is sharing theirs too, and one switch takes you off it.

the door

Everyone here was let in by a real person. Every profile goes through a live selfie before it's looked at. A code comes from a member who's already in, from a surr night, or from the list when there's room.

every day

Being seen is a choice, never a trail. Nobody is shown your hearts, your notes or whose profile you looked at. Block, report and unmatch are one tap away on every profile and in every chat, and a person reads every report. Freeze or delete your account any time.
